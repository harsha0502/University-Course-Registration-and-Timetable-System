Reporting and 
Scenario 
Questions 
(Mandatory) 
Q1. [Reporting] List courses whose waitlist is longer than 20 
percent of the section capacity, with the average waiting time 
of students. (joins, ratio, DATEDIFF) 
Q2. [Reporting] Find rooms used for less than 40 percent of 
available weekly hours and suggest sections that could be 
moved into them. (aggregation, NOT EXISTS) 
Q3. [Reporting] Show the complete prerequisite chain with 
depth for a given course and list all students who already 
satisfy every course in the chain. (recursive CTE, NOT 
EXISTS) 
Q4. [Reporting] List students registered below the minimum 
or above the maximum credits for the term, with the 
difference. (CASE, SUM, HAVING) 
Q5. [Reporting] Show each faculty member's weekly 
teaching hours and the percentage of the limit, ranking the 
most loaded. (SUM, RANK) 
Q6. [Reporting] Detect timetable clashes between any two 
sections registered by the same student, showing both course 
names and time slot. (self join on registrations and 
time_slots) 
Q7. [Reporting] Show fill rate for each section and the rank 
of the section within its course, listing sections below 50 
percent. (ratio, RANK PARTITION BY) 
Q8. [Scenario] A student tries to register for two sections 
that share the same day and time slot. Show the trigger 
blocking the second and the message that names the clashing 
course. (BEFORE INSERT trigger, join) 
Q9. [Scenario] A student registers for a course whose 
prerequisite was passed only as a backlog (failed first). Show 
the check using the latest pass result and the outcome. 
(trigger, subquery on results)

Q10. [Scenario] A student with a confirmed seat drops the 
course while three students wait. Show the first waitlisted 
student promoted, the waitlist positions renumbered and seat 
counts updated in one transaction. (AFTER UPDATE trigger, 
ROW_NUMBER) 
Q11. [Scenario] A student with 22 credits adds a 4-credit 
course with a limit of 24. Show the credit-limit trigger 
rejecting it and how a 2-credit course is accepted. (trigger, 
SUM) 
Q12. [Scenario] A drop is requested on the day after the 
add-drop window closed. Show the trigger rejecting it and the 
special-permission procedure that records dean approval. 
(trigger, procedure)
